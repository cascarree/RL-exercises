Using only a few random seeds can give very different results. In these experiments, DQN's performance changed by more than 20% depending on which three seeds were chosen. The confidence intervals were also quite wide, showing that average scores alone do not tell the full story. Although DQN had a higher average reward than REINFORCE, the statistical tests showed that the difference was not strong enough to confidently say that DQN was better. These results support the paper's argument that RL algorithms should be evaluated using more seeds and proper statistical methods. The most surprising result was how much the choice of seeds could change the final conclusions.
